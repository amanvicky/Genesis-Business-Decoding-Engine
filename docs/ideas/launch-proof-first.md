# Launch: The Proof-First Plan

## Problem Statement
How might GENESIS go from "built and pushed" to "strangers running it and telling
others" — without money, without betraying free/local, and without waiting?

## Recommended Direction
The Meta-Launch. Run GENESIS's own launch through GENESIS: decode "free business
research tool" as the idea, generate the launch playbook from its own tabs, and
publish that dossier as the story. Surround it with the three real dossiers
(ADP, Razorpay, Notion) as proof-first case studies, aim at the Indian-founder
beachhead first (deepest KB), and ship it as a Show HN / r/SideProject post with
the demo GIF above the fold. After launch: weekly build-in-public changelogs.
Idea Battle and Reality Watch become v1.6/v1.7 launch-cycle content, not blockers.

## Key Assumptions to Validate
- [ ] Dossier quality survives expert eyes (2 founders mark up the ADP dossier)
- [ ] Cold install < 15 minutes (one non-adjacent human, timed)
- [ ] Show HN tolerance: real artifacts + honest limitations + no AI-hype framing
- [ ] Free/local reads as a feature (demo GIF above the fold answers this)

## MVP Scope
IN: 3 published dossier case studies, the meta-launch dossier as the launch post,
cold-install test, HN + one Indian-founder community post, weekly changelog page.
OUT: paid ads, Product Hunt same-day, monetization, rebranding, new features first.

## Not Doing (and Why)
- Waiting for v1.6 — secret projects die quietly; the product is launch-ready
- Paid ads — zero budget, wrong stage, and it would betray the zero-cost identity
- Launching everywhere at once — one beachhead, one story, then widen
- KB flywheel — needs the audience this launch creates

## Open Questions
- Which 3 features lead the post? (candidates: verdict protocol, DNA, school)
- Does the repo need a 60-second demo GIF of a full decode at the very top?
- HN title: "Show HN" vs "I built" framing — depends on how the meta-dossier reads
